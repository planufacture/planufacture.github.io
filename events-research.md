# F&B Manufacturing Events — Ireland & UK

Organised by relevance to Planufacture's target market (production planning, scheduling, traceability, automation for food & beverage manufacturers).

---

## Ireland — Primary

### Tier 1: Highest Relevance

| Event | Date | Location | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FoodTech Conference & Exhibition** | **25 Jun 2026** (2027 edition: 24 Jun) | Johnstown Estate, Co. Meath / Leopardstown Pavilion, Dublin | Ireland's premier F&B *manufacturing* event. Core themes: automation, smart factory, traceability, food safety, digital transformation, Industry 4.0. Exact Planufacture audience — plant-floor to C-suite. |
| **National Manufacturing & Supply Chain Conference** | **27–28 May 2026** | RDS Simmonscourt, Dublin | Ireland's largest manufacturing event (cross-sector but heavy F&B presence). Automation, supply chain resilience, digital transformation. Good for broad manufacturing buyer conversations. |
| **Enterprise Ireland Food Innovation Summit** | TBC (annual, Cork) | Cork | Government-backed. Sustainability, food safety, innovation. Access to EI-supported companies — ideal for getting in front of scaling Irish food manufacturers. |

## Recommendations for Planufacture

**Highest-priority events to attend/exhibit:**

1. **FoodTech Ireland (Jun 2026 / Jun 2027)** — bulls-eye audience, themes perfectly aligned (traceability, automation, digital transformation, scheduling)
2. **PPMA Show (Sep 2026)** — largest UK processing & packaging show; production managers actively looking for solutions
3. **Foodex Manufacturing Solutions (Apr 2026)** — core UK F&B manufacturing buyers
4. **National Manufacturing & Supply Chain (May 2026)** — Irish manufacturing decision-makers under one roof

**For dairy sub-sector specifically:** Irish Dairy Forum + Dairy-Tech UK cover both markets.

**For networking/credibility:** FDF membership opens doors to their intimate events (Automation Unleashed, AI & Food Safety) where you'd be in a room with senior leaders from major brands.
